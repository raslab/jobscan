_culture & benefits_
Tech Stack
We work with the latest technologies: artificial intelligence, machine learning, containerization, DevOps, low-code / no-code, blockchain, IoT, AR and many more. You want to work with Go, Rust, TypeScript, JavaScript, Java, Swift, Kotlin or C/C++? Here, too, we have the complete range on offer.
Collaboration
Working with project hubs and subsidiaries around the world makes our work both international and exciting. We believe that collaboration and community make our work and solutions better. That's why we also use FOSS and are members of various open source foundations.
Growing and learning
Through extensive internal and external training opportunities, conferences and trainings, you can deepen your know-how and advance yourself and your team. With Udemy Business, we also give you unlimited access to more than 6,000 online courses – learn whenever and wherever you want.
Mobile working abroad
Freedom and personal responsibility are very important to us! Would you rather work remotely than in the office? Here we are flexible and will find the optimal solution for you and your team. Working where others spend their holiday? No matter whether you prefer to hibernate at the beach or spend your summer in the mountains – with us, you can work from other EU countries for up to 20 days a year!
Flexible working hours
We offer flexible options for organizing your working hours: in addition to 30 days of vacation and independently plannable working hours, you can also convert plus hours into free time. In this way, we increase the compatibility of private and professional life.
Fairness and Diversity
We are committed to equal opportunities, diversity and an inclusive environment for all. This also means that thanks to our agile way of working and the opportunity of leadership tandems, almost all positions are possible on a part-time basis. We regularly review our processes for fairness and equal pay and are pleased to be certified with the Lurse Pay Equity Seal.
Your Career
We promote talent! With innovative processes and a wide range of possibilities, we unleash your potential holistically. Whether Lead Specialist or Manager, we will accompany and support you intensively on your way. In addition, we offer you all the development opportunities that a large corporation has to offer.
People Principles
We are united by our passion for innovation and pioneering spirit. We always look for new ways to grow and improve. We are characterized by fast learning, a feedback-based culture and mutual support.

Our application process
Quickly find the right job! Simply apply online with your CV and any other documents. Our recruiting team then checks your documents and also looks for other jobs that suit your skills.

We want to get to know you and your skills. Depending on the position, we use different methods such as coding challenges, an assessment center or simply an interview. No matter which one, you can be certain that we will always give you fair, transparent and quick feedback.
