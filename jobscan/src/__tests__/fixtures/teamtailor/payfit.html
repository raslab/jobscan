How we hire at PayFit

Discovery Call 🚀

Connect with a Talent Acquisition Specialist to dicuss your expectations and assess how you align with the role and our work culture.

Meet your Future Manager 🤝🏽

Share your vision and skills with your potential manager. Explore the role's challenges and how you see yourself contributing to the team.

    Case study or live exercise 💡

    Complete a case study or exercise to showcase your abilities. We'll evaluate both your approach and results. Feel free to explain your thinking during the discussion.

    Meet your director (optional) ⭐️

    For some jobs, you might meet a company leader to learn more about PayFit's plans and how you could help us grow.

    Preparing to Apply: Your Questions Answered

    • Can I apply for multiple positions at the same time?

      Yes, you can apply for multiple positions at PayFit. We encourage you to apply for any roles that align with your skills and career goals.

    • What can I do to make my application stand out?

      To make your application stand out, start by researching PayFit and explaining why you want to join our team. Submit a well-prepared resume that showcases your relevant skills, experience, and enthusiasm for the role. 

      Carefully read the job descriptions and apply only to positions that match your experience and interests. Tailor your application to highlight how your background aligns with the specific requirements of the role.

    • What are the common mistakes to avoid during the application process?

      Common mistakes include submitting a generic application, not tailoring your resume to the role, and failing to highlight relevant experience. Proofread your application to avoid any spelling or grammatical errors.

    • How does PayFit ensure a diverse pool of candidates during recruitment?

      We use inclusive language in job descriptions, conduct unbiased interviews, and implement strategies to reach a diverse range of candidates.

    • I know someone who works at PayFit. Can they refer me?

      Absolutely! Contact them before you apply. They can submit your information internally, so be sure to send them your resume and any other relevant details they might need.

    What to Expect Next: Your Questions Answered

    • What is the usual duration of the application process?

      While timelines can vary, our goal is to inform candidates within 7 days if we’ll be moving forward with their application. Our recruiters provide updates throughout the process to keep you informed.

    • Will I receive information about PayFit before my first interview?

      Yes, when the recruiter contacts you to schedule the initial call, we send you a candidate guide. This guide includes useful and relevant information about PayFit, our company culture and what to expect during the interview process. It’s designed to help you prepare and feel more confident going into your first interview.

    • What should I expect during the interview process? ﻿

      The interview process typically includes a discovery call with a Talent Acquisition Specialist, a meeting with your potential manager, a case study or live exercise, and optionally, a conversation with a director to reflect on your interest in joining our team.

    • How does PayFit handle feedback and communication during the hiring process?

      We strive to maintain open and transparent communication with all candidates. Our recruiters provide timely updates and constructive feedback throughout the process to ensure a positive candidate experience.
