Digital Product Owner - SAP Sales

Location: 

Wroclaw, PL, 51-502

Position Type:  Professional
Permanent/Temporary:  Permanent

 

Transport is at the core of modern society. Imagine using your expertise to shape sustainable transport and infrastructure solutions for the future. If you seek to make a difference on a global scale, working with next-gen technologies and the sharpest collaborative teams, then we could be a perfect match.

What you will do

Join us at Digital Technology and Operations (DTO) to support the largest SAP transformation at Volvo Construction Equipment!

 

We are an international team of professionals maintaining and implementing new sales capabilities on S/4 HANA platform.

 

We are looking for a Senior Digital Product Owner with hands-on SAP knowledge to lead the digital product delivery in ODC Order Management team.

 

This position seamlessly blends backlog management with SAP solution leadership, collaborating with business stakeholders and SAP consultants. Works in close partnership with the Digital Product Manager to shape the product roadmap and drive business priorities.

 

Senior Digital Product Owner ensures backlog at solution level is well structured, transparent, and understood. Secures that the digital product is developed and maintained in compliance with given architecture, security requirements, and digital product strategy. Fosters open, transparent communication with team members and stakeholders while cultivating trust and promoting a collaborative, supportive environment. Tactfully manages scope by saying no when needed, while constructively addressing challenges, raising risk awareness, and promoting effective planning. Uses SAP expertise to guide other consultants in finding technical solutions that work. Effectively collaborates with SAP architects to answer important design questions.

Who are you?

  • 7+ years hands on expertise in SAP Sales and Distribution area (design, configuration, integration with other components; on ECC and/or S/4 Hana)
  • You acted on implementation projects successfully delivering solutions for Order-to-Cash (O2C) processes (Sales Order Management, Pricing, Billing, Shipping, Returns)
  • You have proven experience in leading a team of SAP professionals to successful outcomes in a role like architect, functional lead or technical lead.
  • You demonstrate a solid understanding of SAP Architecture, Integrations patterns, Enhancement concepts, SAP Fiori and SAP master data.
  • You can effectively read and debug ABAP code.

 

Good to have:

  • VMS and Variant Configuration knowledge
  • Automotive sector experience
  • Hands-on experience in other SAP modules

 

Other Skills:

  • Experience working in a global environment with many different cultures. Excellent online collaboration skills.
  • A self-starter who takes ownership of assigned tasks and drives them to completion
  • Demonstrates structured thinking and is organized at work.
  • Can synthetize complex problems and propose effective solutions.
  • Excellent communication skills
  • Feels at ease with agile ways of working
  • Open to share knowledge and act as a mentor

What’s in it for you?

  • Work in a multi-cultural environment and a very nice team of skilled and dedicated individuals
  • Personal impact on team’s way of working, direction and objectives, with possibility to work independently to drive towards agreed goals
  • Opportunity to grow fast and innovate in SAP area
  • Work in trusting environment, brimming with ideas and willingly supporting each other

Ready for the next move?

Are you excited to bring your skills and disruptive ideas to the table? We can’t wait to hear from you. Apply today! 

We value your data privacy and therefore do not accept applications via mail.

Who we are and what we believe in

We are committed to shaping the future landscape of efficient, safe, and sustainable transport solutions. Fulfilling our mission creates countless career opportunities for talents across the group’s leading brands and entities.

Applying to this job offers you the opportunity to join Volvo Group. Every day, you will be working with some of the sharpest and most creative brains in our field to be able to leave our society in better shape for the next generation. ​We are passionate about what we do, and we thrive on teamwork. ​We are almost 100,000 people united around the world by a culture of care, inclusiveness, and empowerment.

 

Volvo Group Digital Technology & Operations (DTO) is a new division established to integrate the capabilities of VG Digital & IT and VG Connected Solutions to accelerate the digital transformation in Volvo Group. The organizational set up is structured around domains, digital products with functions for digital excellence to deliver outstanding customer experience.

 

Joining the new DTO division means being part of a fast-moving digital product-oriented organization where teams truly own what they build from idea to delivery. In DTO, we work in agile, cross-functional teams, mastering the latest technology, and creating outstanding digital experiences that make a real difference for our colleagues and Volvo Group customers around the world. We put people first and build our culture on trust, passion, customer success, change, and performance. If you want to grow, collaborate across functions and entities, and help shape the future of digital products within Volvo Group, DTO is a great place to be.
