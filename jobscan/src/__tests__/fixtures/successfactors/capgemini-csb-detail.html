.Net Developer

Full Stack .NET Developer
 

Choosing Capgemini means choosing a company where you will be empowered to shape your career in the way you’d like, where you’ll be supported and inspired by a collaborative community of colleagues around the world, and where you’ll be able to reimagine what’s possible. Join us and help the world’s leading organizations unlock the value of technology and build a more sustainable, more inclusive world.

Your Role 

Responsibilities & Scope

  • Work with product owner and team to maintain and prioritize backlog.
  • Design, develop, and maintain data pipelines, data processing infrastructure and services for real-time/batch decisioning, reporting and related functions per best practices.
     
  • Work with stakeholders to understand requirements and influence design architecture.
     
  • Contribute to the overall technical vision and strategy for our data platform.
     
  • Plan and execute software development using Agile processes.
  • Perform root cause analysis of system and data issues and develop solutions as required.
     
  • Be a strong leader to other Developers – lead by doing.

 

Your Profile

  • Must have strong analytical, communication, facilitation, conflict resolution and avid problem-solving skills.
     
  • Build software prototypes and conduct experiments to evaluate alternative architectures/tools/frameworks **C#, ASP.NET, .NET Core, SQL Server, Web API, MVC, Azure App Service, Azure SQL, Cosmos DB, Redis**
     
  • Experience on Azure DevOps or Jira or Service Now.
     
  • Experience building dashboards and distribute to stake holders.
     
  • Experience of both front end and middleware development in cloud environment

What you’ll love

  • Join global employee networks that promote engagement, development, and belonging.
  • Access continuous learning through internal academies, certifications, and mentorship.
  • Well-being hub and different wellbeing initiatives 

 

Need to know

Flexible Schedules: We support flexible working hours to accommodate different lifestyles, time zones, and family responsibilities. 

Career Development in Spanish & English: Training, certifications, and mentorship programs available in both languages to support bilingual growth.
