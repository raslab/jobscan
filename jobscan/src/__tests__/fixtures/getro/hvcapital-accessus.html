About

Accessus is a reliable and innovative company that facilitates clinical research partnerships, enabling practices to conduct studies without disrupting their operations. They provide access to cutting-edge therapies for patients in familiar settings, enhancing the quality of care in Germany. By optimizing existing research centers and supporting new ones, Accessus aims to improve patient outcomes and contribute to the advancement of medical research. Their model allows practices to participate in economically viable research while maintaining high standards of patient care, positioning them as a key player in the healthcare research landscape.
