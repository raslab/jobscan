About

Finmid is the embedded lending infrastructure powering platform growth. Finmid enables platforms to launch tailored financing products for their business customers at scale across 30 European markets via single integration. Across industries and business models, Finmid drives revenue, improves retention, and fuels core business growth. Finmid is trusted by Europe’s most ambitious platforms, including Wolt, Delivery Hero, Bolt, Just Eat Takeaway, Guesty, and FREENOW. Learn more at finmid.com.
